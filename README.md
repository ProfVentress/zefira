# Zefira

A free, private log of rescue inhaler use for people with asthma.

Open it on your phone and add it to your home screen: https://profventress.github.io/zefira/

- One tap records a use; details (relief or prevention, puffs, night waking, symptoms, possible trigger, how much it helped, a note) are optional.
- A calendar shows uses by day and marks weeks with relief use on 3 or more days.
- A summary for your doctor can be printed or saved as a PDF.
- Records are stored only on your phone. There is no account, tracking or server. Back them up with Settings > Save a backup file.

Zefira is a record-keeping tool. It does not give medical advice.
